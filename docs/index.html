How this works

Each emergency type is matched to facility levels assumed able to handle it, and to a target travel time. Travel time and the route come from WHO AccessMod (anisotropic least-cost paths on roads, tracks and open land), precomputed for 335 population-weighted points across the analysis area, for the dry season and a mapped 2026-07-08 flood. All facility capabilities and speeds are labeled assumptions, editable in the repository's config/ files.
